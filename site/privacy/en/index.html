COMO Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-09

1. Information we collect

COMO does not collect personal information or send it to the developer's servers. There is no sign-up, and no ads or analytics tools are used.

2. Information stored in your browser

Settings, favorites, alert rules and portfolio entries are stored only in your browser (chrome.storage) and are never sent elsewhere.

3. Calls to external services

To fetch public data such as prices, exchange rates, indices and news, COMO calls the public APIs of exchanges (Upbit, Bithumb, Binance, etc.) and data providers. It does not read the content of the web pages you visit.

4. Exchange account connection (optional)

A read-only API key you enter yourself is stored only in your browser and sent only to the corresponding exchange. Keys with trading permission are rejected.

5. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not share or sell user information to third parties.

6. Contact

GitHub issues (https://github.com/hunhunseunghun/como_extension/issues) or hunhunseunghun@gmail.com

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
