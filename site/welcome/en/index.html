COMO is installed 🎉

Pin it to your toolbar to see prices in one click.

  1. Click the puzzle-shaped “Extensions” icon to the right of the address bar.
  2. Click the pin (📌) next to COMO. In Edge, click the eye icon, “Show in toolbar”.
  3. Click the COMO icon on your toolbar — a short setup lets you pick an exchange, favorite coins and the toolbar price.

Good to know

  • Press Alt+Shift+C (⌘+Shift+Space on Mac) to open and close COMO.
  • Settings → Side panel keeps COMO open next to the page.
